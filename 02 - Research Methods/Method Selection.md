# Research Method Selection

## Factors from Lecture 1
Use the research question and context to guide the method.

### 1. What is being studied?
- **People:** developers, users, teams, organizations.
- **Technical artefacts:** software, tools, systems, algorithms.
- **Theory:** consequences or properties derived from existing theory.

### 2. How many replications are feasible?
Empirical research can vary greatly in scale:
- Projects/organizations may be difficult to access, so the number of cases may be small.
- Short tasks with many participants may allow many replications.
- Technical artefacts can sometimes be evaluated at very large scale.

### 3. What is the research purpose?
- Explore concepts and identify a hypothesis.
- Validate a hypothesis.
- Determine the state of research / research frontier.

## Dimensions
Research methods can range from empirical to formal and from technology-oriented to people-oriented.

## Course methods introduced
- Human experiments
- Case studies
- Technical experiments
- Formal methods
- Systematic literature reviews
- Mapping studies
- Grounded theory
