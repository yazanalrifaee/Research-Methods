# Chapter 2 — Flashcards

1. **Q:** Why are metrics needed in research? **A:** To characterize observations in a meaningful, measurable, and interpretable way.
2. **Q:** What three measurement questions should be answered? **A:** What to observe; how to determine it; how to quantify it.
3. **Q:** Why can LoC be ambiguous? **A:** Counting rules differ for blank lines, comments, and multiple statements.
4. **Q:** Objective vs subjective metric? **A:** Observer-independent determination vs judgement-dependent determination.
5. **Q:** Direct vs indirect metric? **A:** Directly measured vs derived from other measures.
6. **Q:** Example of an indirect metric? **A:** Productivity = size / effort.
7. **Q:** What is a nominal scale? **A:** Categories with equality as the meaningful relation.
8. **Q:** Example of nominal data? **A:** Defect category.
9. **Q:** What is an ordinal scale? **A:** Ordered categories; order is meaningful, distances are not established.
10. **Q:** Why doesn't assigning 1,2,3 to low/medium/high make it interval? **A:** The category distances may not be equal.
11. **Q:** What is an interval scale? **A:** Ordered values with meaningful equal differences but no true zero.
12. **Q:** Example of interval scale? **A:** Celsius temperature.
13. **Q:** What is a ratio scale? **A:** A scale with interval properties and meaningful absolute zero.
14. **Q:** Example of ratio scale? **A:** Duration or mass.
15. **Q:** What additional operation is meaningful for ratio data? **A:** Ratios, e.g. twice as much.
16. **Q:** What scale are Likert responses? **A:** Strictly ordinal; interval treatment requires assumptions.
17. **Q:** What does GQM stand for? **A:** Goal–Question–Metric.
18. **Q:** Why was GQM introduced? **A:** To avoid collecting large, context-insensitive metric sets that are hard to interpret.
19. **Q:** What is GQM's top-down direction? **A:** Goal → Questions → Metrics.
20. **Q:** What is the bottom-up direction? **A:** Measurements → answers to questions → goal interpretation.
21. **Q:** Name GQM's four phases. **A:** Planning, Definition, Data Collection, Interpretation.
22. **Q:** What are the elements of a GQM goal? **A:** Object, purpose, quality focus, viewpoint, context.
23. **Q:** Why is context important? **A:** It influences which questions and metrics are relevant.
24. **Q:** Can one metric answer multiple questions? **A:** Yes.
25. **Q:** What is the key principle of metric selection? **A:** Derive metrics from goals and questions, not from availability.
